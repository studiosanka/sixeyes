\section{System Overview \& Architecture}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Follower PCB Beta (Rev\,2) is the physical carrier board for \textbf{Node0} of the
SixEyes NodeMesh VLA system. Node0 is the real-time orchestrator providing:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{400\,Hz deterministic control} -- trajectory profiling for four stepper joints
          and three servo joints simultaneously.
    \item \textbf{2-link planar inverse kinematics} -- upper arm / forearm link length
          $= 140\,\text{mm}$ each.
    \item \textbf{Data aggregation} -- synchronous merging of joint-state frames from Node1
          (ESP32-C6 leader board, offboard, connected via hardware UART at 921\,600\,baud)
          and vision feature vectors from Node2 / Node3 (ESP32-CAM modules, wireless
          ESP-NOW on channel\,6).
    \item \textbf{Full TMC2209 feature utilisation} -- StallGuard2 sensorless homing,
          CoolStep adaptive current scaling, SpreadCycle / StealthChop2 mode switching,
          and per-driver UART configuration via PDN\_UART single-wire bus.
    \item \textbf{Telemetry logging} -- MicroSD circular-buffer experience packet logging
          ($\approx 300\,\text{B/packet}$, FAT32).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Module Selection Note -- Octal PSRAM Variant}

The \textbf{ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8} is the required module variant (16\,MB Flash,
8\,MB Octal PSRAM). The octal-PSRAM interface permanently commits the following
GPIO lines to internal silicon use. \warn{These pins must not be routed to any
external pad, via, connector, or pull resistor on the PCB:}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\textbf{GPIO} & \textbf{Internal Function (N16R8 variant)} \\
\midrule
GPIO33 & Flash D4 \\
GPIO34 & Flash D5 \\
GPIO35 & Octal PSRAM D6 / WP \\
GPIO36 & Octal PSRAM D7 / HOLD \\
GPIO37 & Octal PSRAM CLK \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

GPIO26 is additionally used by the internal octal flash clock and must not be routed.

\section{Power Delivery Network (PDN) \& Domain Architecture}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{24\,V Motor Rail}

The four TMC2209 \texttt{VM} pins are fed directly from the 24\,V input bus.
Each driver requires the following filtering placed within $2\,\text{mm}$ of the IC body:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item $100\,\mu\text{F}$ / 35\,V \textbf{low-ESR SMD electrolytic} (Panasonic FR or
          Nichicon UWX series, case size D / $7.3\,\text{mm}$ diameter) -- bulk transient
          reservoir per driver. \warn{Do not substitute tantalum; polarity reversal or
          inrush spikes from motor back-EMF cause catastrophic tantalum failure.}
    \item $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R ceramic, 50\,V (0603) -- high-frequency bypass, trace
          length to pin $\le 1\,\text{mm}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{6.6\,V Servo Domain}

\begin{description}[noitemsep]
    \item[Regulator:] TPS54540B -- 5.5\,A synchronous buck, HSOP-8-EP package. Readily
          available, comprehensive datasheet, budget-friendly.
    \item[Loads:] 3$\times$ MG996R/MG995 digital servos (Wrist Pitch, Wrist Yaw, Gripper).
    \item[Continuous / peak:] 5.0\,A / 7.0\,A stall.
    \item[Inductor:] Shielded iron-powder / metal composite, $L = 4.7\,\mu\text{H}$,
          $I_{\text{sat}} > 9.0\,\text{A}$, footprint $\ge 10\,\text{mm} \times 10\,\text{mm}$
          (e.g.\ Bourns SRR1260 or Sumida CDEP134).
    \item[Output capacitor:] $\ge 1000\,\mu\text{F}$ low-ESR SMD electrolytic (2$\times$
          $470\,\mu\text{F}$ / 16\,V in parallel acceptable) plus $10\,\mu\text{F}$ ceramic bank
          at servo headers.
    \item[Feedback resistors (1\% tolerance, 0603):]
          \begin{equation}
              V_{\text{out}} = V_{\text{ref}} \times \left(1 + \frac{R_{\text{top}}}{R_{\text{bot}}}\right)
              = 0.8\,\text{V} \times \left(1 + \frac{102\,\text{k}\Omega}{14\,\text{k}\Omega}\right)
              \approx 6.63\,\text{V}
          \end{equation}
          Use $R_{\text{top}} = 102\,\text{k}\Omega$, $R_{\text{bot}} = 14\,\text{k}\Omega$
          (0.1\% preferred for tighter setpoint).
\end{description}

\subsection{5.0\,V Logic \& Peripheral Domain}

\subsubsection{Regulator}

\textbf{LMR14030S} -- 3\,A asynchronous buck, HSOP-8-EP package. Common, inexpensive,
well-stocked.

\subsubsection{Load Budget (Worst-Case Simultaneous)}

\begin{tabular}{lrr}

The LMR14030S 3\,A rating provides $>1.3\times$ headroom over worst-case peak.
Inductor: $4.7\,\mu\text{H}$, $I_{\text{sat}} > 3.5\,\text{A}$.
Output: $\ge 100\,\mu\text{F}$ SMD electrolytic + ceramic bank.

\subsubsection{Node1 Leader Board Power Header (J\_LDR)}

Node1 (ESP32-C6 SuperMini, offboard) is powered from the 5.0\,V rail via a 
4-pin JST XH 2.5mm connector (\texttt{J\_LDR}) on the South Edge.
A $10\,\mu\text{F}$ ceramic bypass cap must be placed at the header pins. This header
is \textit{separate} from the UART signal connector (\texttt{J\_LDR}); see
Section~\ref{sec:connectors}.

\subsection{3.3\,V Logic Domain}

\begin{description}[noitemsep]
    \item[Regulator:] \textbf{MIC5219-3.3YM5} (1\,A, SOT-23-5). Replaces the AP2112K-3.3
          (600\,mA) to provide safe headroom. Same SOT-23-5 footprint; drop-in on schematic.
          Available from all major distributors at low cost.
    \item[Input capacitor:] $\ge 10\,\mu\text{F}$ ceramic (0805) on \texttt{+5V} input pin.
    \item[Output capacitor:] $22\,\mu\text{F}$ ceramic (0805) + $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R (0603)
          on \texttt{+3.3V} output pin.
    \item[Loads and peak current budget:]
\end{description}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
\textbf{Load on 3.3\,V rail} & \textbf{Peak (mA)} \\
\midrule
ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 core + RF              & 500 \\
4$\times$ TMC2209 $V_{IO}$ pin (each $\approx$ 15\,mA) & 60 \\
Pull-up resistor networks (10 nets)     & 20 \\
\midrule
\textbf{Total}                          & \textbf{580} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The MIC5219-3.3 1\,A rating provides $\approx 420\,\text{mA}$ of headroom.
Monitor LDO junction temperature ($T_J$) under sustained ESP-NOW burst transmit;
worst-case dissipation $P_D = (5.0 - 3.3)\,\text{V} \times 0.58\,\text{A} \approx 1.0\,\text{W}$.
Ensure adequate copper spreading under the SOT-23-5 pad.

\section{4-Layer Substrate Stackup}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

All SMD components are restricted to \textbf{Layer 1 (Top)} for single-pass PCBA.
Layer 4 carries no components; its copper is used for thermal spreading and signal escape only.

\begin{table}[h!]

\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{llll X}

Standard JLC4L stackup (or equivalent): L1--prepreg--L2--core--L3--prepreg--L4.
Request controlled impedance: $90\,\Omega$ differential for USB pairs on L1.

\section{Component Layout \& Edge Zone Management}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{Perimeter Edge Allocations}

\subsubsection{North Edge -- RF Keep-Out Zone}

Reserved exclusively for the \textbf{ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8} module.
The onboard PCB antenna must overhang the board edge by $\ge 2\,\text{mm}$.
A copper keep-out zone across \textbf{all four layers} ($15\,\text{mm} \times 10\,\text{mm}$)
is mandatory directly below and around the antenna projection.
\warn{No connectors, mounting holes, vias, copper pours, or metallic enclosure features
may occupy this zone.}

\subsubsection{West Edge -- Power \& Communications}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{J\_PWR} -- XT30 right-angle connector, 24\,V DC input / GND.
    \item \textbf{J\_USB1} -- USB-C receptacle, Native USB-OTG (ROS2 bridge / telemetry).
    \item \textbf{J\_USB2} -- USB-C receptacle, CH340K UART debug / firmware flash.
\end{itemize}

Power entry components (bulk electrolytic caps, TVS diode, reverse-polarity MOSFET if fitted)
cluster immediately inboard of the West Edge to minimise high-current 24\,V loop area.

\subsubsection{South Edge -- Actuators, Cameras, \& Leader Link}

All connectors use \textbf{vertical (straight)} orientation to eliminate right-angle
horizontal clearance overhang.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{J\_M1 -- J\_M4} -- JST XH 2.5mm, 4-pin, stepper motor phases
          (one per driver). May wrap to East Edge if South space is insufficient.
    \item \textbf{J\_SV1 -- J\_SV3} -- 3-pin 2.54\,mm header, servo PWM / power / GND
          (Wrist Pitch, Wrist Yaw, Gripper).
    \item \textbf{J\_CAM1} -- 2-pin JST XH 2.5mm, 5\,V / GND for Node2
          (global perspective ESP32-CAM).
    \item \textbf{J\_CAM2} -- 2-pin JST XH 2.5mm, 5\,V / GND for Node3
          (wrist / eye-in-hand ESP32-CAM).
    \item \textbf{J\_LDR} -- 4-pin 2.54\,mm header, Leader UART signals
          (TX, RX, GND, 5\,V) to Node1 ESP32-C6.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{East Edge -- Overflow Stepper Headers}

If stepper headers J\_M3 and J\_M4 cannot fit on the South Edge alongside the servo and camera
connectors, they relocate to the East Edge in the same vertical orientation.

\subsubsection{Interior Zones}

\begin{description}[noitemsep]
    \item[Power Domain (West-Centre):] 6.6\,V and 5.0\,V bucks, MIC5219-3.3 LDO, all bulk
          electrolytic capacitors. Clustered adjacent to the West Edge.
    \item[Driver Matrix (East-Centre):] 4$\times$ TMC2209 QFN-28 in a 2$\times$2 grid.
          Thermal via farms mandatory. Motor phase traces route directly outward to edge headers.
    \item[Logic \& Memory Core (Centre-South):] MicroSD slot and critical decoupling
          passives. Maximises isolation from buck switching nodes.
\end{description}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Passive Decoupling \& Filtering Networks}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{TMC2209 Per-Driver Filtering (Repeat $\times$4)}

All capacitors placed within $2\,\text{mm}$ of IC package edge. Reference TMC2209 datasheet
application circuit throughout.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{VM bulk:} $100\,\mu\text{F}$ / 35\,V low-ESR SMD electrolytic (Panasonic FR /
          Nichicon UWX, case\,D). \warn{Electrolytic only -- no tantalum.}
          Between \net{+24V\_MOTOR} and \net{GND\_D}.
    \item \textbf{VM HF bypass:} $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R 50\,V (0603). Trace to
          Pin\,27 (\texttt{VM}) $\le 1\,\text{mm}$.
    \item \textbf{VIO filter:} $1.0\,\mu\text{F}$ X7R (0603) from \net{+3.3V} to
          Pin\,12 (\texttt{VIO}).
    \item \textbf{Charge pump:} $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R 50\,V (0603) between Pin\,22
          (\texttt{VCP}) and Pin\,24 (\texttt{CPI}).
    \item \textbf{Internal regulator:} $4.7\,\mu\text{F}$ X5R (0603) from Pin\,25
          (\texttt{VOUT}) to GND.
    \item \textbf{DIAG pull-up:} $47\,\text{k}\Omega$ (0402) from Pin\,26 (\texttt{DIAG})
          to \net{+3.3V}. DIAG is open-drain; pull-up is mandatory for valid logic level.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{CH340K Decoupling}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R 10\,V (0402) on \texttt{VCC} supply pin to GND,
          placed $\le 1\,\text{mm}$ from IC.
    \item $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R 10\,V (0402) on \texttt{V3} (internal 3.3\,V reference
          pin) to GND.
    \item $100\,\text{nF}$ X7R (0402) on \texttt{XI} USB signal conditioning pin
          per CH340K datasheet recommendation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 Module Decoupling}

Per Espressif hardware design guidelines for the WROOM-1 module:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item $10\,\mu\text{F}$ + $100\,\text{nF}$ ceramic on the \texttt{3V3} module supply pin
          cluster to GND, placed directly adjacent to the module pad row.
    \item Place decoupling capacitors \textit{in-route}: power plane via $\to$ capacitor
          pad $\to$ IC supply pin. Do not branch off a parallel stub.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{MicroSD Card Slot}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ pull-up resistors on \net{SD\_CS}, \net{SD\_MOSI},
          \net{SD\_MISO} to \net{+3.3V}.
    \item $100\,\text{nF}$ bypass cap on VCC supply rail of card slot to GND.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Node1 / Node2 / Node3 Power Header Bypass}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item $10\,\mu\text{F}$ ceramic cap on 5\,V at each of J\_CAM1, J\_CAM2, J\_LDR\_PWR
          headers to absorb ESP-NOW TX current burst ($\approx 200\,\text{mA}$ step).
\end{itemize}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{High-Density Layout \& Thermal Rules}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{In-Route Decoupling Rule}

Decoupling capacitors must be placed \textit{in-series} with the power trace,
not tapped off a stub:

\[
  \text{Power Plane Via} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{Cap pad (0402/0603)} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{IC supply pin}
\]

This eliminates the inductance of a parallel stub branch. Applies to all TMC2209 VM
caps, all 3.3\,V / 5\,V decoupling on the ESP32-S3 module pads, and the CH340K VCC cap.

\subsection{TMC2209 Thermal Via Farms}

Each TMC2209 QFN-28 must dump its entire thermal load through the PCB substrate.

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item Place a $3 \times 3$ via farm inside the exposed centre ground pad
          (\texttt{EP}) of each driver footprint.
    \item Drill diameter: $0.3\,\text{mm}$. Grid pitch: $\le 1.0\,\text{mm}$.
    \item Vias must connect L1 $\to$ L2 (solid GND plane) and extend to L4.
    \item On Layer 4, draw a solid copper pour matching the TMC2209 driver grid
          footprint area with no solder mask (\texttt{B.Mask} cutout in KiCad).
    \item This converts the PCB bottom surface into a passive thermal radiator.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Buck Switching Node Isolation}

The \texttt{SW} nodes of both buck converters, their freewheeling diodes, and
inductor pads must be routed as ultra-wide, short copper polygons on Layer 1.

\warn{No signal trace on L1 or L4 may pass beneath these switching node polygons.}
Affected critical nets: ESP-NOW RF path (Node2/Node3 timing), SPI MicroSD transactions,
Leader UART receive path (\net{LDR\_UART\_RX}).

\subsection{Ground Stitching}

Place GND stitching vias (L1 $\to$ L2):
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Board perimeter: every $\approx 5\,\text{mm}$.
    \item Along the full length of both USB differential pair routes.
    \item Immediately adjacent to all buck regulator switching polygons.
    \item Between each power domain zone boundary on L3.
\end{itemize}

Servo high-current GND return must not share any trace segment with MCU signal GND.
Both must terminate at the L2 GND plane via \textit{separate} vias before any
common-mode coupling path can form.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Peripheral Interface Notes}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{Wireless Camera Nodes (Node2 / Node3 via ESP-NOW)}

J\_CAM1 and J\_CAM2 supply \textbf{5\,V power and GND only}. All data is exchanged
wirelessly:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Protocol: ESP-NOW, channel\,6, stateless packet send.
    \item Payload: 128-byte brightness histogram feature vector embedded in
          \texttt{ExperiencePacket} (magic \texttt{0x4E4D5650}).
    \item Freshness gate: Node0 discards frames older than 300\,ms before IK merge.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Leader UART -- Node1 ESP32-C6 (Offboard)}

Node1 is an ESP32-C6 SuperMini board connected via two separate connectors:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{J\_LDR\_PWR}: 5\,V / GND power (JST XH 2.5mm, 2-pin, South Edge).
    \item \textbf{J\_LDR}: UART signals TX / RX / GND / spare (2.54\,mm 4-pin, South Edge).
\end{itemize}
Baud: 921\,600, 8N1, non-blocking async receive. Parser uses magic-number scan +
CRC-CCITT (poly \texttt{0x1021}) before packet consumption. Packets carry absolute
joint target values for all 6 joints.

\subsection{Dual USB-C Interface}

\begin{description}[noitemsep]
    \item[Port 1 -- J\_USB1:] Native USB-OTG. $90\,\Omega$ differential pair directly
          on GPIO19 (D$-$) / GPIO20 (D$+$). Used for ROS2 USB-CDC bridge, telemetry
          streaming, VLA dataset logging.
    \item[Port 2 -- J\_USB2:] CH340K UART bridge. GPIO43 (TXD) / GPIO44 (RXD).
          DTR/RTS auto-reset for PlatformIO upload. Eliminates need for external
          USB-to-serial programmer during development.
\end{description}

\subsection{MicroSD Card}

Hardware SPI. FAT32, 32\,kB clusters. 1\,MB pre-allocated circular buffer for
experience packets. SD flush every 8 writes to reduce blocking.
Pull-ups: $10\,\text{k}\Omega$ on MOSI, MISO, CS lines.

# 3. Print the mechanics

**Status: coming soon.**

This guide will cover the printed parts for the arm and the 1:25 cycloidal gearboxes: the files, print
settings, and what to buy to go with them (bearings, fasteners).

The printable files will be published in [`hardware/mechanical/stl/`](../hardware/mechanical/stl/) when they
are ready.

Useful background in the meantime: the [gearbox torque budget](reference/GEARBOX_TORQUE_BUDGET.md) explains

The bearing, insert and screw lists written for this guide will also fix the last unpriced lines of the
[cost budget](reference/BUDGET.md).
